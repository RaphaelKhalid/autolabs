# Application baseline

## Positioning

Lead with operational ownership in technically complex work. AutoLabs is useful evidence because it shows the candidate turning an open research question into a bounded, observable, approval-gated program. Keep the scientific scope honest: the study is a protocol and working record, not a confirmed result.

## Best application thesis

I make complicated research work legible and dependable. I build the documentation, status boundaries, evidence links, and process improvements that let researchers stay focused while keeping claims and decisions reviewable.

## Mapping to the role

| Constellation need | AutoLabs evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Project plans and documentation | Stable experiment descriptors, research handoffs, protocol pages, and explicit status states |
| Cross-team coordination | Separate public observatory, study-specific runner, source question, artifact links, and approval gate |
| Feedback and iteration | Fast protocol and interface revisions, including steering controls, candidate limits, progress status, and evidence boundaries |
| Process improvement | Read-only public surface, bounded contracts, malformed-state tests, and no silent promotion of planned work |
| Complex technical topics | SAE feature discovery, persona vectors, activation steering, held-out confirmation, and evaluator limits |
| Reliable communication | Clear distinction between planned, active, completed, and unverified work |

## Scientific framing

The paper identifies prompt-elicited persona extraction as supervised and coarse, then presents SAE decomposition as a complementary route to finer-grained features. The application should describe the follow-on as testing whether label-free SAE candidates support repeatable, cross-context behavioral hypotheses. It should not say the study discovered personas or produced a confirmation result.
