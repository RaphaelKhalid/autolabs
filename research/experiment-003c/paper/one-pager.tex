\textbf{Research question.}
Can an SAE identify coherent persona-relevant directions without a prespecified
target trait? I investigate the discovery question raised in the limitations
section of Chen et al.\ (2025). Prompt reachability was omitted from the final
run; ease of prompt elicitation is reserved for future work.

\textbf{Method.}
I train a Matryoshka BatchTopK SAE on $100$M assistant-position tokens from
\texttt{Qwen2.5-\allowbreak{}7B-\allowbreak{}Instruct} at decoder layer $19$ (zero-based). It has $32{,}768$
features, approximately $235$M parameters, held-out FVE $0.717$, and mean $L_0=40$.
Candidate selection uses assistant-specificity, density-quantile, and
prompt-informed arms. Both signs are steered across $24$ scenarios, alongside
$20$ random directions and three supervised persona-vector controls. A blinded
language-model judge describes selected steered-baseline pairs.

\textbf{Recorded outcomes.}
The diagnostic criterion $G_0$ combines residual separability and cross-scenario
consistency. It is satisfied by $60/128$ features and two of three controls. Judge
evaluation covers $40$ selected signed feature directions and three random
directions. Ten signed candidates receive consistent speaker-related descriptions
above the naming-null threshold; all ten also pass $G_0$. They represent
\textbf{nine distinct features}, because both signs of feature $2036$ are named.

\textbf{Interpretation.}
The two candidates selected without persona prompts are confounded by degraded
output. Feature $148+$ produces only punctuation in all $24$ scenarios. Its
``engaged and supportive'' label describes the baseline response in all $48$
judge comparisons, not the steered output. The coherence proxy nevertheless
accepts $23/24$ responses, exposing a validity problem. Feature $717+$, labelled
``composed and confident,'' passes that proxy on only $8/24$ scenarios and also
shows substantial degradation. The eight other named directions come from
prompt-informed selection. The current evidence therefore does not establish
coherent unlabelled persona discovery.

\textbf{Limitations and next research steps.}
The study uses one model and layer, selective judge coverage, unmatched random
perturbation norms, and three naming nulls selected for low separability.
Reconstruction replacement yields $78\%$ argmax agreement, below the configured
$90\%$ threshold. Future evaluation should require substantive coherent output,
retain judgement polarity, use comparable nulls, and include independent human
validation. Prompt-elicitation difficulty should be measured after coherent
candidate effects have been established.
